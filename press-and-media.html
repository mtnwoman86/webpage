Press & Media

Ulyana Horodyskyj Peña's work at the intersection of science, exploration, climate change, and public engagement has been featured in television, documentary film, print, podcasts, and digital media.

Featured Film & Television

Ages of Ice

PBS | 2026

Ulyana appears in the PBS documentary series Ages of Ice, filmed on location in New Zealand, in Episode 1: The Frozen Heart. The series explores Earth's frozen environments and the profound role ice has played in shaping our planet, its climate, and human history.

Learn more

The Andes Tragedy: 50 Years Later

2023

Ulyana appears alongside her husband, mountain guide Ricardo Peña, and Flight 571 survivor Eduardo Strauch in this documentary revisiting the 1972 crash of Uruguayan Air Force Flight 571 and its extraordinary story of survival. The documentary follows a return expedition to the remote crash site and explores Ricardo's longstanding connection to the survivors and the discoveries he has made there.

View on IMDb

TEDx Talk

How Do We Climb Out of Crisis? Learn from Snow and Catastrophe

TEDxBoston | 2024

In her TEDx talk, delivered at the MIT Planet Action Summit, Ulyana draws on a lifetime spent studying snow and ice, from childhood encounters with glaciers to scientific expeditions in some of the world's most extreme environments. She explores how pollution accelerates the loss of snow and ice, and how lessons from both science and human resilience can help us confront the climate crisis with reason, action, and hope.

Selected Podcasts & Interviews

Geographies of Hope: “When the ICE Speaks: Where Glaciologist Ulyana Horodyskyj Peña Finds Hope”

2026

Ulyana joins geographer and explorer Dr. Karen Barton for a conversation about glaciers, exploration, climate change, and finding hope amid the profound environmental changes taking place across Earth's frozen landscapes. Drawing on her experiences as both a scientist and explorer, Ulyana reflects on what the ice can teach us and how firsthand encounters with a changing planet can inspire curiosity, connection, and action.

Listen on Spotify

Wild World with Scott Solomon: “Tracking Glaciers in the Andes with Ulyana Horodyskyj Peña”

2025

Ulyana joins evolutionary biologist and science communicator Dr. Scott Solomon for a wide-ranging conversation about shrinking glaciers, climate change, and her experiences studying ice in mountain environments around the world. The episode explores her path into glaciology, creative approaches to conducting field research, and why the loss of Earth's glaciers matters far beyond the mountains.

Space4U Podcast: “Dr. Ulyana Horodyskyj – Science in the Wild Founder”

2020

In this Space Foundation interview, Ulyana discusses her Himalayan glacier research, citizen science and high-altitude fieldwork, as well as her experience commanding NASA's HERA XII simulated deep-space mission. The conversation also explores women in science, the parallels between extreme environments on Earth and human spaceflight, and her own interest in space exploration.
