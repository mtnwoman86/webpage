Speaking

Dr. Ulyana Horodyskyj Peña is an engaging keynote speaker who brings audiences into some of the most extreme environments on Earth through a unique combination of science and exploration. A professional scientist, National Fellow of The Explorers Club, former commander of NASA's HERA XII mission, mountaineer, and founder of Science in the Wild, Ulyana has spoken to audiences around the world, from the TEDx stage to guest speaking aboard PONANT expedition ships in the polar regions. Her talks draw on more than a decade of fieldwork and expeditions across all seven continents to make science, exploration, and leadership accessible and personal.

Extreme Science

What is it like to conduct science at the ends of the Earth, and what can these remote places tell us about a rapidly changing planet? Ulyana takes audiences behind the scenes of field science, from working on the back deck of an icebreaker in Antarctica and collecting pollution samples across the Arctic Ocean to studying snow pollution in the Canadian Arctic and paddling across glacial lakes in Nepal to investigate flood hazards. Her expeditions have taken her as far as the Geographic North Pole and into the remote Andes, where she has helped guide expeditions with her husband, Ricardo Peña, to the wreckage site of Uruguayan Air Force Flight 571, the remarkable survival story made famous by Alive and Society of the Snow.

Climbing & Science

Climbing mountains is challenging both physically and mentally. Conducting science at high altitude adds another dimension entirely. Drawing on expeditions in mountain ranges around the world, Ulyana shares stories of science and exploration from the Himalaya, Mount Kilimanjaro, Aconcagua, Ojos del Salado, the European Alps, and beyond. Her work explores everything from changing glaciers and glacial hazards to the presence of human-made pollutants in some of the world's highest and most remote environments, demonstrating how exploration can become a powerful platform for scientific discovery and public engagement.

Leadership in Extreme Environments

What happens when four strangers are locked together inside a habitat the size of a small apartment for 30 days? As commander of NASA's HERA XII (Human Exploration Research Analog) mission at Johnson Space Center, Ulyana led a four-person crew through a simulated deep-space mission designed to help NASA better understand the physical and psychological challenges of long-duration spaceflight. She shares lessons about leadership, teamwork, communication, conflict, resilience, and decision-making under pressure and how those lessons translate far beyond space exploration.

Women in Science & Exploration

Ulyana's path into science began with science fairs as a young student and eventually took her from the classroom to a Ph.D., the world's highest mountains, the polar regions, and a NASA simulated space mission. Along the way, she has navigated the challenges of building a career across science, exploration, education, and communication, fields in which women have historically been underrepresented. Drawing on her own experiences, Ulyana speaks about the importance of mentorship, opportunity, perseverance, and creating pathways for the next generation of women in STEM and exploration.

Science, Exploration & Storytelling

Science doesn't have to stay inside laboratories, academic journals, or conference halls. Ulyana's work has increasingly focused on finding creative ways to bring science to wider audiences, whether through field expeditions, public speaking, educational programs, documentary storytelling, or television. Drawing on experiences including her TEDx talk, guest speaking aboard PONANT expedition voyages, and appearances in documentary and television productions, she explores how scientists and explorers can turn data and discovery into stories that help people connect with our changing planet.
