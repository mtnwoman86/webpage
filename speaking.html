Speaking

Dr. Ulyana Horodyskyj Peña is an engaging keynote speaker who brings audiences into some of the most extreme environments on Earth through a unique combination of science and exploration. A professional scientist, National Fellow of The Explorers Club, former commander of NASA's HERA XII mission, mountaineer, and founder of Science in the Wild, Ulyana has spoken to audiences around the world, from the TEDx stage to guest speaking aboard PONANT expedition ships in the polar regions. Her talks draw on more than a decade of fieldwork and expeditions across all seven continents to make science, exploration, and leadership accessible and personal.

Remote Science

Field science from Antarctica and the Arctic Ocean to glacial lakes in Nepal, and expeditions in the remote Andes connected to the 1972 Flight 571 survival story.

Climbing & Science

Stories of science and exploration from the Himalaya, Kilimanjaro, Aconcagua, Ojos del Salado, and the Alps, where climbing becomes a platform for discovery.

Leadership

Lessons from commanding NASA's HERA XII analog space mission: teamwork, resilience, and decision-making under pressure.

Women in Science

Mentorship, perseverance, and pathways for the next generation of women in STEM and exploration.

Science for the Public

Turning field science into stories for wider audiences, from the TEDx stage to documentary and expedition speaking.
